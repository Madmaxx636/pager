# Privacy

Pager has no company behind it and no servers of its own. There is no account with me, no telemetry, no analytics and no advertising.

- **Your data lives on your server.** Messages, contacts you import, settings and recovery-key backups are stored there and nowhere else.
- **Contact import:** if you import contacts, names and numbers are saved to your own account on your own server so your devices can match
  them. They are not sent anywhere else.
- **What other services see:** each bridge talks to its service (WhatsApp, Signal, and so on) as you, so that service sees your use as it
  would from any linked device. A free DuckDNS name means DuckDNS learns your server's address. GIF search (if you add your own key)
  sends your search words to Giphy or Tenor.
- **Updates:** the apps ask your own server whether a newer version exists. Nothing is sent to anyone else.
- **Crash details** stay on the phone until you choose to copy them.

If you run a server for other people, you are the one who can see their data. Tell them what you do with it.
